# Day 11/90: Exploring and improving my portfolio

**Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Class day:** no

## Problem
After looking at a few developers' portfolios, I was impressed by how they presented their work, experience and skills. It made me realise that sometimes we have the experience and skills, but communicating our value is where we struggle. My current portfolio no longer reflects my personality, experience or the kind of developer I'm becoming.

## Solution
I took a different approach today and decided to revisit my portfolio instead of waiting for the "perfect time". I'm working on its design, structure and presentation to make it a better representation of who I am and what I can do.

## Goals for the portfolio
- Reflect my personality, experience and the kind of developer I'm becoming
- Present my work, experience and skills clearly
- Be something I can continuously improve
- Showcase new projects as I build them
- Support my freelancing journey

## Lesson
- Having skills isn't enough. Communicating their value is a skill of its own.
- Applying what you learn shouldn't always wait for the perfect time.
- I've always enjoyed learning, experimenting and improving, and a portfolio should keep improving with me.
